# Brief

Northstar Notes is a fictional note-taking app. The support lead needs a morning triage report for the requests in `requests.csv`.

The report should help a human support lead decide what to do next. Group related requests when useful, but do not hide individual request IDs. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions. Flag anything that needs a product, billing, or security decision.

Write three short response drafts for the most urgent requests. These are drafts only; nothing may be sent externally.
